The 2026 FIFA World Cup was held in **Canada, Mexico, and the United States** from June 11 to July 19, 2026. Sixteen host markets staged 104 matches: two in Canada, three in Mexico, and eleven in the United States. Spain won the final 1–0 after extra time against Argentina at New York New Jersey Stadium.

That answer needs one important qualification: FIFA’s “host city” labels often described a metropolitan market, not the municipality printed on a stadium’s street address. Boston’s venue was in Foxborough, Dallas’s in Arlington, and New York New Jersey’s in East Rutherford. The list below separates the tournament label from the physical location.

## The country split

FIFA’s [official tournament overview](https://www.fifa.com/en/tournaments/mens/worldcup/canadamexicousa2026/articles/fifa-world-cup-2026-hosts-cities-dates-usa-mexico-canada) lists the three-country distribution:

| Host country | Host markets | Share of the 16 markets |
|---|---:|---:|
| Canada | 2 | 12.5% |
| Mexico | 3 | 18.75% |
| United States | 11 | 68.75% |

It was the first men’s World Cup organized across three countries. The expanded 48-team format produced 104 matches rather than the 64 used in the previous 32-team tournament.

## Where the opening match and final rounds were played

The geography was not evenly interchangeable. FIFA assigned the tournament’s signature matches to specific venues. The scores below follow FIFA's [completed fixtures and results](https://www.fifa.com/en/tournaments/mens/worldcup/canadamexicousa2026/articles/match-schedule-fixtures-results-teams-stadiums); FIFA also published dedicated reports for the [bronze match](https://www.fifa.com/en/tournaments/mens/worldcup/canadamexicousa2026/articles/france-england-report-highlights-bronze-final) and [final](https://www.fifa.com/en/tournaments/mens/worldcup/canadamexicousa2026/articles/spain-argentina-final-report-highlights).

| Stage | Date | Venue | What happened |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opening match | June 11 | Mexico City Stadium | Mexico beat South Africa 2–0 |
| First semifinal | July 14 | Dallas Stadium | Spain beat France 2–0 |
| Second semifinal | July 15 | Atlanta Stadium | Argentina beat England 2–1 |
| Bronze match | July 18 | Miami Stadium | England beat France 6–4 |
| Final | July 19 | New York New Jersey Stadium | Spain beat Argentina 1–0 after extra time |

Mexico City Stadium became the first venue to stage a men’s World Cup opening match for the third time, after 1970 and 1986. New York New Jersey Stadium hosted the final in East Rutherford, not in New York City. The [official match-schedule announcement](https://www.fifa.com/en/tournaments/mens/worldcup/canadamexicousa2026/articles/fifa-world-cup-26-match-schedule-revealed) also records that Dallas received nine matches, the most of any host market.

## Why the host map was organized in regions

The venue network stretched from Vancouver to Mexico City and from the San Francisco Bay Area to Boston. It was not realistic to treat all 16 markets as stops on one continuous road trip.

FIFA said the majority of matches were arranged in eastern, central, and western zones, and that three rest days were preserved for teams in 103 of the 104 matches. That regional logic reduced some travel, but progression still moved teams between cities. A bracket path was therefore also a travel path.

Looking back, the cleanest way to reconstruct the tournament is to pair three records:

1. the official results for opponents and scores;
2. the FIFA venue name for the historical match record;
3. the physical municipality for maps, transport, and local archives.

## The short answer

World Cup 2026 took place across **16 host markets in three countries**: Toronto and Vancouver in Canada; Guadalajara, Mexico City, and Monterrey in Mexico; and Atlanta, Boston, Dallas, Houston, Kansas City, Los Angeles, Miami, New York New Jersey, Philadelphia, the San Francisco Bay Area, and Seattle in the United States.

The tournament opened in Mexico City and ended in East Rutherford, New Jersey, where Spain became world champion on July 19, 2026.
